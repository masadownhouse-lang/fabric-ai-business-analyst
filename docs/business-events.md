# Business Events (MVP)

This document describes the first business events that will be generated into the synthetic dataset.

The purpose is to create realistic business scenarios for KPI analysis, variance analysis and root cause analysis.

---

## Event 001 - Purchase Price Increase

Description

Raw material purchase prices increase over time.

Expected Impact

- COGS increases
- Gross Margin decreases
- EBITA decreases

Typical AI Question

Why did Gross Margin decrease?

---

## Event 002 - Promotion Campaign

Description

A temporary sales campaign increases volume but lowers profitability.

Expected Impact

- Revenue increases
- Quantity sold increases
- Gross Margin % decreases

Typical AI Question

Why did revenue increase while profitability decreased?

---

## Event 003 - Demand Drop

Description

Demand decreases for one product category.

Expected Impact

- Sales decrease
- Inventory increases
- Inventory Turnover decreases

Typical AI Question

Why did inventory value increase?

---

## Event 004 - Inventory Expiry

Description

Products reach their expiry date and must be written off.

Expected Impact

- Write-Offs increase
- Inventory Value decreases
- EBITA decreases

Typical AI Question

Which products generated the highest write-off costs?

---

## Event 005 - Product Recall

Description

A product batch is recalled.

Expected Impact

- Inventory write-offs
- Customer impact
- Potential revenue loss

Typical AI Questions

Which customers were impacted by the recall?

What was the financial impact of the recall?
