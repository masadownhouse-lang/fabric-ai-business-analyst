# Event 002 Root Cause Analysis

## Business Event

Promotion Campaign

## Root Cause Path

Promotion Campaign
↓
Campaign Discounts
↓
Revenue Increase

Promotion Campaign
↓
Campaign Discounts
↓
Gross Margin % Decrease

Promotion Campaign
↓
Campaign Cost
↓
Operating Expenses Increase

Operating Expenses Increase
↓
EBITDA Decrease

## Expected AI Questions

Why did Revenue increase?

Why did Gross Margin % decrease?

Why did EBITDA decrease despite higher sales?

## Expected AI Conclusion

Revenue increased due to higher sales volume during the promotion campaign.

However, Campaign Discounts and Campaign Costs reduced profitability, resulting in lower Gross Margin % and lower EBITDA.
