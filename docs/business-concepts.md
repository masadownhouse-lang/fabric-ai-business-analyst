# Business Concepts

## Product

A sellable inventory item.

Examples

- Greek Yogurt 200g
- Cheddar Cheese 1kg
- Pepperoni Pizza 300g

Related Entities

- Supplier
- Lot
- Sales Invoice
- Purchase Invoice

---

## Lot

A traceable batch of products.

Purpose

Supports:

- Product Recall
- Product Expiry
- Inventory Traceability

---

## Customer

Organization purchasing products.

Segments

- Retail
- Wholesale
- Restaurant
- Public Sector

---

## Supplier

Organization supplying products.

Supplier Categories

- Dairy Supplier
- Beverage Supplier
- Frozen Food Supplier
- Packaging Supplier

---

## Product Recall

Removal of affected product lots from market.

Business Impact

- Inventory Write-Off
- Revenue Impact
- Customer Impact

---

## Purchase Price Increase

Increase in supplier purchase prices.

Business Impact

- COGS Increase
- Gross Margin Decrease
- EBITDA Decrease
---

## EBITDA

Definition

Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization.

Formula

Revenue
- COGS
- Operating Expenses

Business Meaning

Measures operating profitability before financing and accounting adjustments.

Root Cause Drivers

- Revenue Changes
- Purchase Price Changes
- Inventory Write-Offs
- Marketing Costs
- Personnel Costs

Related Concepts

- Revenue
- COGS
- Operating Expenses
- Gross Margin
