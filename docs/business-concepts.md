# Business Concepts

## Product

A sellable inventory item.

Examples

- Greek Yogurt 200g
- Cheddar Cheese 1kg
- Pepperoni Pizza 300g

Related Entities

- Supplier
- Lot
- Sales Invoice
- Purchase Invoice

---

## Lot

A traceable batch of products.

Purpose

Supports:

- Product Recall
- Product Expiry
- Inventory Traceability

---

## Customer

Organization purchasing products.

Segments

- Retail
- Wholesale
- Restaurant
- Public Sector

---

## Supplier

Organization supplying products.

Supplier Categories

- Dairy Supplier
- Beverage Supplier
- Frozen Food Supplier
- Packaging Supplier

---

## Product Recall

Removal of affected product lots from market.

Business Impact

- Inventory Write-Off
- Revenue Impact
- Customer Impact

---

## Purchase Price Increase

Increase in supplier purchase prices.

Business Impact

- COGS Increase
- Gross Margin Decrease
- EBITDA Decrease
---
---

## EBITDA

Definition

Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization.

Formula

Revenue
- COGS
- Operating Expenses

Business Meaning

Measures operating profitability before financing and accounting adjustments.

Root Cause Drivers

- Revenue Changes
- Purchase Price Changes
- Inventory Write-Offs
- Marketing Costs
- Personnel Costs

Related Concepts

- Revenue
- COGS
- Operating Expenses
- Gross Margin

Related Accounts

Revenue

- 3000 Sales Revenue Finland
- 3010 Sales Revenue Sweden
- 3020 Sales Revenue Estonia

COGS

- 4000 Raw Materials
- 4010 Dairy Raw Materials
- 4020 Frozen Food Raw Materials
- 4030 Beverage Raw Materials
- 4100 Packaging Cost
- 4200 Freight Cost

Operating Expenses

- 5000 Warehouse Personnel Cost
- 5010 Sales Personnel Cost
- 5020 Administration Personnel Cost
- 5100 Warehouse Cost
- 5110 Cold Storage Cost
- 5200 Marketing Cost
- 5210 Campaign Cost
- 5300 Administration Cost
- 5400 Inventory Write-Off
- 5410 Expiry Write-Off
- 5420 Recall Write-Off

Example Analysis

If EBITDA decreases:

1. Analyze Revenue variance
2. Analyze COGS variance
3. Analyze Operating Expense variance
4. Identify largest contributing account groups
5. Identify largest contributing account categories
6. Quantify impact in euros
7. Map findings to known business events
