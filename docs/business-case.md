# Business Case

## Company

Nordic Foods Group

A fictional food manufacturing and distribution company operating in:

- Finland
- Sweden
- Estonia

---

## Core Processes

- Sales
- Purchasing
- Inventory
- Finance

---

## Business Objectives

The company wants to improve business decision making by using Microsoft Fabric and AI.

Key goals:

- Understand why KPI changes occur
- Identify root causes behind profitability changes
- Improve inventory management
- Analyze product recalls and their impacts
- Detect products at risk of expiry

---

## Key Business Questions

- Why did EBITA decrease?
- Why did Gross Margin decline?
- Why did Inventory Value increase?
- Which products are likely to expire?
- Which customers were impacted by a product recall?

---

## Planned AI Capability

An AI Business Analyst capable of:

- KPI analysis
- Variance analysis
- Root cause analysis
- Inventory insights
- Recall impact analysis
