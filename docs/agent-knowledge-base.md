# Agent Knowledge Base

## Root Cause Pattern 001

Purchase Price Increase

Indicators

- Increased purchase prices
- Increased raw material costs
- Increased COGS

Expected Impacts

- Gross Margin decreases
- EBITDA decreases

Detection Logic

Purchase Price Increase
↓
COGS Increase
↓
Gross Margin Decrease
↓
EBITDA Decrease

## Root Cause Pattern 002

Promotion Campaign

Indicators

- Increased sales volume
- Increased discounts
- Increased campaign costs

Expected Impacts

- Revenue increases
- Gross Margin % decreases
- EBITDA decreases

Detection Logic

Promotion Campaign
↓
Campaign Discounts
↓
Gross Margin % Decrease

Promotion Campaign
↓
Campaign Cost
↓
Operating Expense Increase
↓
EBITDA Decrease
